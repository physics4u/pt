      Quick start· the first five minutes
      1. Sign in with the username and password your admin gave you. The very first time, the owner signs in as admin with the owner passcode.
      2. Check the year. The gold chip at the top right shows the academic year (for example 2026–27). Admins can change it in Settings → Academic year.
      3. Add students on the Students page with + Add student. Set each student's class (Plus 1 or Plus 2) and monthly fee.
      4. Plan the month in Monthly Planner: pick the chapter and times for each class day.
      5. Take attendance in Attendance. Everything saves automatically.
      6. Record fees from a student's profile (Students → click their name).
      7. The Dashboard adds everything up for you.
      Signing in, roles and your account

      Signing in

      Open PT at the web address your admin shared with you and enter your username and password. You stay signed in on that device for 30 days. The chip at the top right shows who is signed in; click it to go to Settings.

      Roles

      UserStudents, classes, Monthly Planner, attendance, payments and scores.
      AdminEverything a User can do, plus Users, chapter lists, the academic year and backups.
      OwnerThe built-in admin sign-in that uses the owner passcode. It always works, so the owner can never be locked out.

      Change your password

      Settings → Users → Change my password. Enter your current password and a new one (at least 6 characters).

      Forgot your password?

      Ask an admin. They open Settings → Users, hover over your row and click ✎, type a new password and press Save changes.

      Sign out

      Settings → Users → Sign out. Do this on shared or borrowed computers.

      Dashboard

      Click the PT logo at any time to come back here.

      • Active students: students whose status is Active and whose end date (if any) hasn't passed.
      • Classes: how many weekly classes are on the Classes page.
      • Revenue: group fees marked Paid plus hourly payments received this academic year. Outstanding: group fees marked Pending or Overdue plus unpaid hourly balances.
      • Hourly hours: total one-to-one session time this academic year.
      • Plus 1 / Plus 2 Hours and Days: teaching time and class days from the Monthly Planner. Hours are shown as hours:minutes, so 134:45 is 134 hours 45 minutes.
      • Teaching Hours charts: time spent on each chapter (Plus 1 in red, Plus 2 in orange).
      • Income — Monthly: paid fees for each month of the academic year.
      Students

      Add or edit a student

      Press + Add student (or hover over a row and click ✎). First name is required; everything else is optional but useful:

      • Class: Plus 1 or Plus 2. Attendance, the planner and payments use this.
      • Batch: Regular, or Hourly for one-to-one students billed by the hour (see Hourly students).
      • Monthly fee: filled in automatically when you create that month's payment.
      • Status: Active, Trial or Inactive.
      • Start / End date: once the end date has passed, PT marks the student Inactive automatically.

      Find students

      Use the search box (name) and the filters for class, batch and status.

      Student profile

      Click a student's name anywhere in PT (Students, Attendance, Payments, Scores) to see everything about them: details, attendance month by month, payments and scores. This is also where you record fees (see Payments).

      Delete a student

      Press Del and confirm. Their earlier attendance and payment records are kept.

      Tip: if you change a student's name, their earlier attendance and payments stay under the old name. Correct spelling mistakes early.

      Hourly (one-to-one) students

      Set up

      Add or edit the student and choose Batch: Hourly. Enter their rate per hour. If they also come to the regular group classes, tick Also attends group classes; they then appear in group attendance and monthly fees as well.

      Record a session

      Open the student (Students → click their name), press + Add session, and enter the date, start and end time, chapter and notes. The hours are worked out for you, and the day counts as present in their attendance.

      Each session is billed at the rate in force when it was added, so changing the rate later doesn't alter earlier bills.

      Record a payment

      Press + Add payment and enter the date and amount received. Payments don't have to match a month: PT applies them to the oldest unpaid months first and shows each month as Paid Part-paid or Due, with the balance at the top.

      Where they appear

      • Payments: an Hourly students table for the grade shows rate, hours, billed, paid and balance.
      • Dashboard: Hourly hours counts their sessions; Revenue and Outstanding include them. The Teaching Hours charts show group classes only.
      Classes

      The weekly timetable. Press + Add class and enter a name, day, level (Plus 1 or Plus 2), start and end time, and optionally tutor, room and fee. Hover over a class and click ✎ to change it; Delete this class is at the bottom of that form.

      Monthly Planner
      1. Choose the grade and the month at the top.
      2. For each day you teach, pick the start and end time and the chapter. Add notes if you like (for example the topics covered).
      3. That's it: each change saves automatically.

      Days with an entry are highlighted; weekends are tinted. The Dashboard's hours, days and Teaching Hours charts come from this page.

      The chapter list comes from Settings → Chapter lists (admins can edit it).

      Attendance
      1. Choose the grade, the month, and whether to show active or all students.
      2. Click a day's box for a student to mark it: first click = P (present), second click = A (absent), third click = blank. Each click saves automatically.

      The P and A columns count each student's month; the bottom row counts each day.

      Payments

      See who has paid

      The Payments page shows every student against every month of the academic year: Paid Pending Overdue. Filter by grade or status. Click a name to open that student.

      Record a payment

      1. Open the student (Students → click their name, or their name on the Payments page).
      2. In the payments table, click the month's status to change it: blank → Pending → Paid → Overdue → blank.
      3. Check the amount, and add the payment date and any notes. These save automatically.

      You can also change the student's monthly fee from the profile.

      Scores

      Scores is organised by test. The page lists the tests for Plus 1 and Plus 2: test number, date, portions, marks out of, the class average, the top mark and how many took it.

      Add a test

      Press + Add test, choose the class, date, portions and marks out of. PT numbers the test for you (Test 12, Test 13…) and opens its mark sheet.

      Enter marks

      Press ✎ Marks on a test. Every active student in the class is listed (tick Show inactive students to see the others); type each mark and press Enter to move to the next student. Marks save automatically and the percentage fills in. Leave a mark blank if the student didn't take the test (it shows "---"). Saved marks are locked: hover over the row and press Edit to change one.

      Change or delete a test

      On the mark sheet, change the test number, date, portions or marks out of under Test details. Delete this test is at the bottom of that box.

      One-to-one tests

      For hourly students, choose For: One-to-one: <student> when adding a test. These are listed separately under One-to-one tests.

      Each student's tests, marks and percentages also appear on their profile.

      Settings· admins see every section
      • Users: everyone who can sign in, with their role and last sign-in. Admins use + Add user, and hover over a user and click ✎ to rename, change role, reset a password, stop someone signing in (untick Can sign in) or delete them.
      • Chapter lists (admins): the chapters offered in the Monthly Planner. Press Edit, put one chapter per line, then Save. "Use the standard NCERT list" fills the box with the built-in list; nothing changes until you save.
      • Academic year (admins): PT shows 14 months, March to the following April. Choose the year and press Save year.
      • Storage (admins): how much of PT's space is used, with a breakdown by area. Free up space releases empty space.
      • Import data (admins): bring in an earlier year's records from its Excel workbook. Check shows the totals to compare with the workbook; Import loads them after making a backup.
      • Backups (admins): Back up now saves a complete copy of PT's data. The newest 30 backups are kept.
      • Appearance: Dark or Light. This is remembered on each device separately.
      Saving and your data
      • Everything you enter is stored securely in PT's online database. Only people with a PT sign-in can see or change it.
      • Every change saves by itself within a second or two. There are no Save buttons to remember.
      • While saving, the bottom of the side menu briefly shows Saving…. If something goes wrong it shows ⚠ Not saved: check your internet connection and try again.
      • If you close the page just after a change, PT finishes saving it. Your browser only warns you if a save has failed (for example when you're offline).
      • PT keeps a copy on your device so it opens quickly, then refreshes it from the database. If the database can't be reached, PT shows the copy but won't save until it reconnects, so nothing is ever overwritten by mistake.
      • PT keeps an activity log of each change and who made it.
      Troubleshooting & FAQ

      "Wrong username or password"

      Usernames are not case-sensitive, but passwords are. Ask an admin to reset your password if needed.

      "Your session has ended"

      Sign-ins last 30 days, and end straight away if an admin disables or deletes your account. Sign in again.

      I don't see an update that was announced

      Reload the page. If it still looks old, press Ctrl + Shift + R (Mac: Cmd + Shift + R). The version in About PT tells you which release you have.

      A student is missing from attendance or payments

      Check their class is set (Plus 1 or Plus 2) and the status filter at the top of the page.

      The Teaching Hours chart leaves out some days

      The chart only counts days whose chapter appears in the current chapter list, with both a start and an end time.

      Something looks wrong in the data

      Tell an admin. If needed, PT's data can be restored from one of the backups.
