      Academic year

      Every page (Dashboard, Students, Attendance, Monthly Planner, Payments, Scores and profiles) shows the year chosen here. The current year runs 14 months, March to the following April; an earlier year shows March to February, because its last March and April are the start of the next year. PT always opens on the current academic year; choosing another year here only changes what you see, not anyone else's view.

      Progress over the years
      Google Calendar

      Each class on the Classes page goes into a PT Classes calendar: weekly classes repeat until the end of the academic year (April), one-off classes happen once. The tutor is invited by email and the class appears in their Google Calendar with a reminder 30 minutes before. Changing or deleting a class updates the calendar.

      For the reminder on the tutor's phone: in the Google Calendar app, open Settings → (their account) → Default notifications → Events and choose 30 minutes before, and accept the first invitation (Yes).

      Time zone

      PT uses this time zone for "today" (the current week, today's date, the current academic year, end dates) and for the times it shows. It applies to everyone using PT.

      Storage
      Checking…
      #AreaSpace usedShare

      PT can hold up to 10 million cells. Every row and column reserved for an area counts, even when empty; PT trims empty space each time it saves, and Free up space trims everything at once. One academic year of data uses roughly 10,000 cells.

      Import data· bring in records from earlier years

      Save the year's workbook as Excel, named like Physics Tuition - 2025-26.xlsx, anywhere in the Physics Tuition folder (the year's own folder is fine). Check shows what it contains without changing anything. Import adds that year's students, attendance, planner, fees, hourly sessions, payments and marks to PT as that year's own records; other years are not changed. Importing the same year again replaces its student records and adds nothing twice. A fee set in another currency (e.g. 15 OMR) is noted on the student, and fees paid stay in rupees. A backup is made first. Years can be imported in any order.

      Backups· complete copies of PT's data
      Each backup is a complete copy of PT's data, labelled with the date and time. PT keeps the newest 30 backups.

      Quick start· a typical week in PT
      1. Sign in with the username and password your admin gave you.
      2. Students: add each student once with + Add student (class, batch, fee or hourly rate).
      3. Monthly Planner: after each class, enter the start and end time and the chapter.
      4. Attendance: mark the day; Mark everyone present then change the absentees.
      5. Fees: open the student (click their name) and record the month or the payment.
      6. Scores: + Add test, then type the marks.
      7. Dashboard (click the PT logo) adds it all up. Everything saves by itself.
      How PT pages work· sections, locked entries, active students, academic year
      Phones & tablets
      On a small screen the menu hides behind ☰ at the top left. Tap it to open the menu; choosing a page (or tapping beside the menu) closes it. Wide tables scroll sideways.
      Sections
      Most pages are split into sections you open and close (Plus 1, Plus 2, Hourly, the planner's weeks…). Pages open with them closed; the heading shows a summary so you often don't need to open it.
      Locked entries ✎
      Anything already saved is locked so it can't be changed by accident. Hover over it (on a phone or tablet the ✎ is always shown) and press ✎ to change it. Delete / Clear (in red) appear only while editing.
      Names
      Click a student's name anywhere to open their profile.
      Active students
      Lists show only active students. Tick Show inactive students on any page to see everyone; the choice applies to every page until PT is closed.
      Academic year
      The chip at the top right (e.g. 2026–27) is the year shown everywhere. PT always opens on the current year. Click the chip (or Settings → Academic year) to look at another year; the chip turns red while you're viewing a different year.
      Saving
      Every change saves within a second or two. There is no Save button to remember.
      Signing in, roles and passwords

      You stay signed in on a device for 30 days. Settings → Users has Change my password (at least 6 characters) and Sign out (use it on shared computers). Forgot your password? An admin resets it from the same section.

      Students and profiles

      Add or change a student

      + Add student, or hover over a student and press ✎. Only the first name is required. Class (Plus 1 / Plus 2) decides where they appear; Batch is Regular or Hourly; Status is Active, Trial or Inactive. Once the End date passes, PT marks the student Inactive. Delete this student is at the bottom of the form; their old attendance and fees are kept.

      Each academic year has its own student list

      Each academic year has its own student records: a new year starts with no students, and every student added to a year is a new record for that year (earlier years' records are never reused). A student who attends group classes and one-to-one classes has two records with the same name, one under Plus 1/Plus 2 and one under Hourly; each can end separately (e.g. stopped group classes, still hourly). A student has left when their end date has passed. The same name can't be added twice to the same list in one year.

      Find a student

      Students are grouped into Plus 1, Plus 2, Hourly (Plus 1) and Hourly (Plus 2). Typing in the search box opens the sections with a match; a search includes inactive students. If nothing matches (e.g. a typo like "Sarena"), PT suggests the closest name.

      Profile

      Details, attendance by month, fees, sessions and payments (hourly), and the report card, each in its own section. Attendance, Fees and Report card start with a strip showing the PT logo, the student's name, class, academic year and today's date, so a screenshot is easy to identify. 📷 Share on those sections makes a picture of the section: on a phone it opens the share menu (e.g. WhatsApp to the parent); on a computer it downloads the picture. The ✎ on Student details opens the edit form.

      Changing a student's name leaves their earlier attendance and fees under the old name, so fix spellings early.

      Hourly (one-to-one) students

      Choose Batch: Hourly and enter the rate per hour. Tick Also attends group classes if they come to the group classes too; they then also appear in group attendance and monthly fees.

      On the profile, + Add session (date, times, chapter, notes) and + Add payment (date, amount). Each session is billed at the rate when it was added. Payments go to the oldest unpaid months first, so months show Paid Part-paid or Due. Sessions count as present days. To change the rate, hover over it on the Hourly fees heading and press ✎.

      Monthly Planner

      Choose Plus 1 or Plus 2 and the month (◀ ▶ step a month); the line above the weeks shows the month's class days and hours. The month is split into weeks with the current one open (This week). For each class day pick the start and end time and the chapter, and add notes. A day locks when you move to another row; ✎ reopens it, with Done and Clear. Week headings show days, hours and chapters covered. Chapters come from Settings → Chapter lists.

      Attendance

      Choose Plus 1 or Plus 2 and the month; the whole month is shown in one table, with a thicker line before each Monday. Click a student's box on the day: P → A → blank. The bar above the table shows the day you're marking, with Mark everyone present, Clear day and Done. Marked days are locked; hover over the date and press ✎ to change one. P, A and % are the student's totals for the month; the bottom row counts who was present each day.

      Payments and fees

      Payments shows every student against every month of the year: ₹ paid Pending Overdue. Tick Only students with dues to see who owes fees. Hover over a cell for the payment date and notes.

      Record a fee: click the student's name → Fees section → hover over the month → ✎ → choose the status, check the amount and date → Save. A month marked Paid without a date gets today's date; Clear month undoes it. The monthly fee itself is changed with the ✎ on the Fees heading.

      Scores

      Tests are listed under Plus 1, Plus 2 and the two Hourly sections (one-to-one tests), with average, top mark and how many took each.

      + Add test: class (or One-to-one: a student), date, portions and marks out of; PT numbers it. On the mark sheet type each mark and press Enter for the next student; blank means the student didn't take it. Once a test has marks the sheet opens locked; Edit on a row changes or adds a mark. Test details are locked too: ✎ next to the title edits them and shows Delete this test.

      Classes

      The timetable. + Add class: name, Repeats (every week, or once on a date), day or date, level, times, tutor, room. ✎ on a class changes or deletes it. Students shows the active students at that level.

      When Google Calendar is turned on (Settings), each saved class also goes into the PT Classes calendar with a reminder 30 minutes before, and the tutor is invited by email; changes and deletions update the calendar. 📅 next to the room means the class is in the calendar.

      Settings
      Users
      Who can sign in, their role and last sign-in. Admins add users and use ✎ to rename, change role, reset a password, block or delete.
      Chapter lists
      (Admins) The planner's chapters, one per line. Use the standard NCERT list refills it; nothing changes until Save.
      Academic year
      (Everyone) Show an earlier or later year everywhere in PT. Only your view changes, and PT opens on the current year again next time. Back to the current year returns.
      Progress over the years
      (Admins) Every academic year side by side: fees received (group and hourly), students, teaching hours and fees per student, with growth on the year before, average yearly growth and the best year. The current year is shown lighter as “so far”. Same point last year compares this year with last year up to today’s date (and a chart shows every year up to that date). 📷 Share makes a picture of the whole section to send.
      Google Calendar
      (Admins) Turn on, enter the tutor's Google email, Save, then Add all classes now. The owner allows calendar access once the first time (PT shows a message if it hasn't been allowed). For phone reminders the tutor accepts the first invitation and sets Google Calendar's default event notification to 30 minutes.
      Time zone
      (Admins) Used for "today": the current week, today's date, end dates, the current academic year and times shown. Default India (IST).
      Storage
      (Admins) Space used, by area. Free up space releases empty space.
      Import data
      (Admins) Add a year's records from its Excel workbook (2021–22 onwards). Check shows totals to compare with the workbook's Home screen; Import adds the year as its own student records (nothing is shared with other years), replaces them if the year is imported again, and takes a backup first. Then view it with Academic year.
      Backups
      (Admins) Back up now saves a full copy; the newest 30 are kept.
      Appearance
      Dark or Light, and the font (Modern or Courier New). Remembered on each device.
      Saving and your data
      • Data is stored securely in PT's online database; only people with a PT sign-in can see it.
      • The side menu shows Saving… briefly. ⚠ Not saved means the connection dropped: check the internet and try again.
      • Closing the page right after a change is fine; PT finishes saving. The browser warns you only if a save failed.
      • PT keeps a copy on your device so it opens quickly. If it can't reach the database it shows that copy but won't save, so nothing is overwritten.
      • Every change is recorded in an activity log with who made it.
      Troubleshooting & FAQ
      Wrong username or password
      Passwords are case-sensitive (usernames aren't). Ask an admin to reset it.
      Your session has ended
      Sign-ins last 30 days, and end at once if an admin disables your account. Sign in again.
      A student is missing
      They may be inactive: tick Show inactive students. Also check their class is set, and the academic year chip.
      Pages look empty
      Check the year chip. A red chip means you're viewing another year; click it and choose Back to the current year.
      The week or "today" looks a day off
      An admin can check Settings → Time zone.
      Planner hours missing from charts
      Only days with a chapter and both times are counted.
      Don't see a new update
      Press Ctrl + Shift + R (Mac: Cmd + Shift + R). About PT shows your version.
      Something looks wrong in the data
      Tell an admin; data can be restored from a backup.
